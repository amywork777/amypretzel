# Coffee cup and saucer

Geometry adapted from **Diffuse Transmission Teacup**, by **Polyhaven.com and Eric Chadwick** (2023), from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets collection.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/DiffuseTransmissionTeacup
- Original GLB: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/main/Models/DiffuseTransmissionTeacup/glTF-Binary/DiffuseTransmissionTeacup.glb
- Asset license: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Adaptations for amypretzel: welded and simplified geometry with preserved normals (7,000 cup triangles, 3,000 saucer triangles); removed texture maps and material extensions in favor of an ivory glaze; added a coffee surface fitted to the cup's interior at 58 mm above the original origin. The cup and its handle remain the original joined geometry.

The resulting self-contained GLB is 190,160 bytes and needs no external textures or compression decoder. It is served locally by this website.
